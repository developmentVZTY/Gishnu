How a project runs

From your specification to a working gearbox

Our major strength is the ability to customise an existing standard product into a new design that meets industry-standard requirements. Every project follows the same four stages.

  1. Specification

    We secure the complete specification for the application from you: duty, speeds, loads, mounting and environment.

  2. Design

    Our qualified engineers design the gearbox, often by customising an existing standard product into a new design.

  3. Manufacture

    Gears are cut, ground and machined in our own facility, using our own production technology.

  4. Inspection

    Gear geometry, dimensions and hardness are checked before the gearbox is assembled and dispatched.

Applications

Gearboxes we have engineered to order

Z-Series project details

  • Screw-jack gearboxes

    Gearboxes that drive screw jacks, where rotary input becomes controlled linear lift. Engineered around the load, travel speed and mounting of the jack system.

  • Cooling tower gearboxes

    Gearboxes for cooling tower drives, designed for the continuous running and installation position that the tower requires.

  • Tyre changer gearboxes

    Gearboxes for tyre changing machines, built to the machine maker’s envelope and supplied as a volume product.
