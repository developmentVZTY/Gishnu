Company

Three decades of gear manufacturing in Coimbatore

Founded in 1995 as M.M Gears (P) Ltd and known as Gishnu Gears since 2011, we are an ISO 9001:2015 certified gearbox manufacturing company.

We make bevel gears, helical gearboxes, worm gearboxes and other specialised gear solutions for industrial, automotive and textile customers across India and in international markets. Coimbatore’s airports and ports put those markets within easy reach.

About Gishnu Gears

From drawing to dispatch, under one roof

  1. Design and development

    An in-house design team works from your duty, speed and mounting requirements, including turning a standard gearbox into a new design.

  2. Gear cutting

    CNC gear hobbing on a Nimble NOAH 400H, gear shaping on a Nidec ST25 and horizontal broaching.

  3. Gear grinding

    Gear grinding on a Höfler Helix 400, with a dedicated worm grinding machine for worm shafts.

  4. Machining

    CNC turning and thread milling for shafts, housings and components.

  5. Inspection

    A Klingelnberg P40 gear measuring centre, an Accurate Spectra CMM, and Brinell and Rockwell hardness testing.

  6. Assembly and delivery

    Quality checking and delivery departments sit within the same facility as manufacturing.
