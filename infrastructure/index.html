Infrastructure

Industrial gear manufacturing facility in Coimbatore

A 50,000 sq. ft integrated facility across three units, equipped with the machines to design, cut, grind and inspect gears in-house.

The facility

Built for high-precision, durable products

Total area
50,000 sq. ft
Manufacturing units
3 units
Design, manufacturing, quality and delivery
4 departments

Our infrastructural facility is spread across an area of 50,000 sq. ft over three different units. We are equipped with state-of-the-art machinery and highly configured machines that are ideal for manufacturing high-precision, durable and quality products.

The integrated manufacturing facility is divided into units that comprise design and development, manufacturing, quality checking and delivery departments. With a highly skilled technical team and an in-house design team, we are able to meet the growing demands of our clients.

Our technocrats stay close to the changing demands of the industry and keep their eyes and ears open to new processes, so that the way we manufacture keeps improving.

How the facility is organised

Four departments, one flow

  1. Design and development

    An in-house design team turns application requirements into manufacturable gearbox designs.

  2. Manufacturing

    Gear cutting, grinding and machining on state-of-the-art, highly configured machines.

  3. Quality checking

    Gear measurement, dimensional inspection and hardness testing before assembly and dispatch.

  4. Delivery

    A dedicated department that prepares and ships orders to customers in India and abroad.
