Careers

Careers at Gishnu Gears

Join us. Let’s find the solution together. We are a gear engineering company in Coimbatore where design, manufacturing and quality work side by side.

Where you could work

Four departments under one roof

Our facility brings every stage of gearbox manufacture together, so people here see the whole product, not one corner of it.

  1. Design and development

    Gear and gearbox design within our in-house design team.

  2. Manufacturing

    CNC gear hobbing, shaping, grinding, turning and assembly.

  3. Quality

    Gear measurement, CMM inspection and hardness testing.

  4. Delivery

    Preparing and dispatching finished orders to customers in India and abroad.
