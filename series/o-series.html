Overview

What the O-Series is for

High-precision special gears are transmission components made for critical applications across industries such as aerospace, automotive, medical devices and precision machinery. They are what keeps motion smooth and accurate in vehicle transmissions, medical equipment and precision machines.

The O-Series was created for international markets, where gears and gear spares have to meet demanding standards of quality and precision. It is built on tight-tolerance machining and is supplied for high-pressure industrial machinery, hydraulic systems and export-oriented heavy engineering projects.

Use caseSpecial gears for export markets that require durable, precise performance.
