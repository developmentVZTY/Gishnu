About the Bevel helical gearbox

Bevel helical gearboxes are part of the M-Series of customised industrial gearboxes. Each unit is designed by our engineers around the client’s specification, so shaft arrangement, ratio and mounting follow the machine rather than a catalogue.

They serve the same sectors as the rest of the series: specialised machinery, prototype equipment, automotive projects and industrial automation.
