Gishnu G-Series: powering innovation in industry

The G-Series represents our commitment to high-quality, reliable power transmission. This is how the engineering and manufacturing behind it are organised.

By the Gishnu Gears engineering teamUpdated 23 November 20262 min read

The G-Series represents Gishnu’s commitment to providing high-quality, reliable and innovative power transmission solutions. By leveraging advanced engineering and manufacturing techniques, we ensure that each gearbox delivers the performance and durability its application needs. This is how that works in practice.

Engineering that starts with the application

Every gearbox exists to do a job on a particular machine. Our in-house design team works from the duty, speed and mounting the application requires. Where a catalogue unit is close but not quite right, one of our strengths is customising an existing standard product into a new design that meets the requirement.

Made under one roof

Our facility covers 50,000 sq. ft across three units, organised into design and development, manufacturing, quality checking and delivery departments. Keeping these together means a gear can be cut, finished, measured and assembled without leaving our control.

The machinery behind the G-Series includes:

  • CNC gear hobbing on a Nimble NOAH 400H,
  • gear shaping on a Nidec ST25,
  • gear grinding on a Höfler Helix 400, and dedicated worm grinding,
  • CNC turning and thread milling for shafts and components.

Measured, not assumed

Precision is only real when it is measured. Gear geometry is checked on a Klingelnberg P40 gear measuring centre and component dimensions on an Accurate Spectra coordinate measuring machine. Brinell and Rockwell hardness testers verify that heat-treated parts have reached the hardness the design calls for. We are an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, and this is the equipment that stands behind the certificate.

Where the series is at work

G-Series gearboxes drive conveyor systems, material handling equipment, packaging machinery and agricultural machinery, along with household grinders, water pumps and food processing machinery. The range covers ratios from 5 to 70 and motors from 0.5 to 30 HP.

From standard to special

The same engineering and machines that produce the G-Series also produce our made-to-order work. When an application needs something no series provides, it becomes an M-Series custom-built gearbox or a Z-Series non-standard gearbox, designed from the specification up.
