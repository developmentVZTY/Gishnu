About Gishnu Gears

A gearbox manufacturing company built on precision

From Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, we engineer custom-built gear solutions for industrial, automotive, textile and nuclear applications.

Who we are

Engineering power, precision and performance

1995
Became Gishnu Gears
2011
Quality management
ISO 9001:2015

Welcome to Gishnu Gears, a gearbox manufacturer in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, specialising in custom-built gear solutions for industrial, automotive, textile and nuclear applications. With decades of expertise in gear engineering, we deliver high-performance gearboxes that meet the most demanding requirements.

Founded in 1995 as M.M Gears (P) Ltd, we evolved into Gishnu Gears in 2011, focusing on precision gear engineering, industrial gearbox manufacturing and custom power transmission solutions. As an ISO 9001:2015 certified company, we ensure that every gearbox we manufacture meets global quality standards.

Our CNC technology and precision machining processes enable us to produce bevel gears, helical gearboxes, planetary gear systems, worm gearboxes and other specialised gear solutions with durability and efficiency built in. You can see the machines that make this possible on our infrastructure page.

We serve industries across India and in international markets, using Coimbatore’s strategic location for shipping through accessible airports and ports. Our commitment to innovation, quality and customer satisfaction has earned us a loyal client base in the automotive, textile and heavy engineering industries.

At Gishnu Gears we do not just manufacture gearboxes. We engineer power, precision and performance to drive industries forward. Whether you need a standard industrial gearbox or a customised power transmission solution, we aim to be your partner for quality-driven engineering.

Our global growth story

Building relationships beyond India

At Gishnu Gears we have always believed that precision, performance and trust are the cornerstones of long-term success. These principles helped us build a strong reputation in the Indian market, and they now power our expansion on the global stage.

Recognising a changing world

Our international journey began with a clear recognition of change. Global manufacturers were looking beyond traditional supply hubs for partners who could offer reliability, agility and engineering excellence. As supply chains diversified, especially under the “China Plus One” strategy, we positioned ourselves as a credible, high-performance alternative in gear manufacturing.

Where demand was rising

We identified Europe, Southeast Asia and North America as regions where demand for customised, high-quality gear solutions was growing. Mid-sized manufacturers there were actively seeking suppliers who could match their pace, meet their technical requirements and offer consistent value. That is where we stepped in, launching customisation in 2022 with the Z-Series for gearboxes and the O-Series for gear spares.

Solutions, not just components

With deep expertise in customised gearboxes for industrial automation, mobility and defence, we aligned our strengths with global market needs. Our focus is on delivering solutions that are precision-engineered, performance-tested and built to last, designed to meet evolving international standards while remaining flexible to each customer’s application.

Ready for what comes next

Demand for intelligent, energy-efficient and durable gear systems continues to grow, from automated machinery to electric vehicles and smart manufacturing. We are building a robust foundation through a diversified customer portfolio, digital infrastructure and flexible, scalable operations, so that we remain agile and ready for opportunities across international markets.

To our partners, collaborators and future investors: we invite you to be part of this journey. Together, we can power the world, one gear at a time.
