Overview

Reliable aeration, hour after hour

Our aerator gearboxes are engineered to deliver reliable performance in demanding environments such as aquaculture ponds, wastewater treatment plants, biogas plants and aeration basins. Each unit is built with precision components and heavy-duty materials for long-term durability and low maintenance.

In prawn and shrimp culture, the gearbox turns the motor’s speed into the slow, steady rotation of the paddle-wheel sets. The paddles break the water surface and generate the aeration bubbles that keep dissolved oxygen at the level the stock needs.

All gearboxes are tested under load conditions before dispatch, and Gishnu Gears is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company. Optional add-ons include an anti-rust epoxy coating, integrated torque arms and IEC motor adaptability.

Questions

Aerator gearbox FAQs

Answers to what buyers ask us most often. For anything else, call +91 99439 17774.

Which aerator gearbox models do you manufacture?

Our A-Series has four models: the A1 (ratio 14.4, 1 HP), the A3 (ratio 11 to 13.7, 1 to 3 HP), the A4 (ratio 12.6, 2 to 3 HP) and the GBH 200 (ratio 11 to 13.7, 3 HP).

What is an aerator gearbox used for?

In prawn and shrimp culture, the gearbox drives the paddle-wheel sets that generate aeration bubbles and raise oxygen levels in the pond. The same kind of drive is used for aeration in wastewater treatment plants, biogas plants and aeration basins.

How are the gearboxes protected against water?

They use double-lip oil seals and a labyrinth construction to prevent the ingress of water and dust in moist environments.

Can an aerator gearbox be customised?

Yes. Units can be tailored for shaft size, mounting configuration, paint coating and gear profile. Optional add-ons include an anti-rust epoxy coating, integrated torque arms and IEC motor adaptability.

Are the gearboxes tested before dispatch?

All gearboxes are tested under load conditions before dispatch. Gishnu Gears is an ISO 9001:2015 certified company.

Do you supply aerator gearboxes outside Tamil Nadu?

Yes. We serve customers across India and in international markets, shipping from Coimbatore through its nearby airports and ports.
